# Instructional-Design
This repository showcases the process and application of some of my work in Learning & Development (L&D), Instructional Design (ID), and curriculum development from 2008 until the present.

My projects emphasized scalable, measurable, and standardized learning solutions, demonstrating proficiency in both the agile Successive Approximation Model (SAM) and the foundational ADDIE Model.

Original work or content cannot be shared due to the confidential nature of the content.

## 1. PRIMARY CASE STUDY: Scalable L&D Strategy & Content Governance (6 years)
**Scope:** Senior Technical Writer, Senior Learning & Development Strategist, Content Governance Lead for NTT DATA <br>
**Description:** Led L&D transformation as the organization scaled rapidly (from 300 to 17,000+ employees) through mergers & aquisitions, requiring a unified, standardized training and knowledge infrastructure.<br>
**Model:**: SAM and ADDIE

**SKILL SUMMARY** <br>
**Category** | **Expertise** | 
| ----- | ----- | 
| **Performance Impact** | Reduced onboarding time by 60%; Supported growth from 300 to 15.000+ users; Delivered bespoke learning paths. | 
| **ID Models** | Agile iterative/rapid development (SAM) and fixed content (ADDIE). | 
| **L&D Strategy** | Curriculum Mapping, L&D Strategy, Content Governance, Knowledge Management and Change Management. | 
| **Technical Alignment** | Standardized processes aligned with ITIL, ISO, CIS, and SOC frameworks; Collaboration and validation with cross-functional Subject Matter Experts. | 
| **Content Platforms** | E-Learning Development, Corporate Training, and Centralized Knowledge Management (Confluence). |
| **Software and Tools** | Confluence, Articulate RISE 360, Microsoft Office, Workday, Jira, Photo and Video Editing. |

**CHALLENGES AND RESTRAINTS** <br>
Rapid growth through multiple (and overlapping) mergers and aquisitions created significant strategic and organizational challenges, process inconsistencies, and inefficient employee onboarding. The challenge was to centralize, standardize, and scale all training and knowledge systems in line with the PMO (Project Management Office) in charge, and pivot where and when required.

**KEY RESULTS** <br>
* Reduced onboarding times by an average of 60% by replacing general onboarding training with custom learning paths by department or knowledge area within Operations and Delivery (between 10- and 15000 users).<br>
* Improved clarity and efficiency for up to 15.000 direct and 3.,000 indirect global users by developing and maintaining standardized knowledge and training systems.<br>
* Instrumental in scaling up standards across the organization by curating and maintaining a centralized knowledge repository in Confluence.
  
**PROCESS & METHODOLOGY** <br>
A. SAM Phase 1: Preparation (Evaluation and Needs Analysis) <br>
* Analyzed organizational inefficiencies and knowledge gaps; aligned solutions with critical industry standards (ITIL, ISO, CIS, SOC).<br>
* Designed bespoke learning paths and functional courses tailored to specific Operations and Delivery roles (Curriculum Mapping).<br>
* Researched content and background to fill knowledge gaps when the SME or stakeholder had limited availability for structured and efficient feedback/validation sessions.<br>

B. SAM Phase 2 & 3: Iterative Development and Governance <br>
* Developed and maintained user-centric, self-paced training materials using clear, globally accessible language to improve comprehension of complex processes.<br>
* Orchestrated strategic content initiatives through effective cross-functional stakeholder engagement, directly supporting key organizational strategies and vision.<br>
* Managed Workday Learning administration and reporting, driving release management, and governance and KPI adherence. <br>
* Led the resolution of a high-priority vendor portal access issue, successfully restoring access for 3.000 daily users and enabling 1.500 new user onboardings.<br>

## 2. SUPPORTING CASE STUDY: E-Learning Platform Development (1 year) <br>
**Scope:** Instructional Designer for Interskill Learning and Freelance, in Beijing (CN) and London (UK)<br>
**Description:** E-Learning content creation for online platforms serving diverse, highly regulated clients (leading UK banks, government agencies, and large retailers).<br>
**Model:** ADDIE

**CHALLENGES AND RESTRAINTS** <br>
Rapidly develop engaging, platform-specific e-Learning modules for clients in highly regulated industries with fixed content scope. Creating graphic design cues for imaging and interactive content without direct access to the developers nor the client. 

**KEY RESULTS** <br>
* Conducted audience analysis to inform content and interactive engagement strategies, ensuring high completion rates.<br>
* Created new content, edited existing material, and authored additional chapters to meet varied client training needs.<br>
* Integrated graphic design and creative solutions to deliver professional, user-friendly training modules on fixed client platforms.

## 3. SUPPORTING CASE STUDY: Bespoke Cultural Training & Curriculum (2 years)
**Scope:** Corporate Language & Culture Trainer, Translator for Executive Communications and Freelance, in Beijing (CN) <br>
**Description:** Designed and facilitated custom B2B language and cultural training programs for multinationals in Media, Manufacturing, and Technology.<br>
**Model:** ADDIE

**CHALLENGES AND RESTRAINTS** <br>
Developing custom, mission-critical training (covering language, culture, and business etiquette) for employees transferring internationally, mitigating direct risk to career placement and business relations. Creating language and teaching materials catered to different levels, ages, and learning styles with minimal feedback on what they are and a one week production delivery timeline after the initial meeting. Supporting the creation of audit and compliance material in English with limited understanding of the industry and/or corporate background. Supporting walk throughs in English based on information provided wholly in Mandarin Chinese. 

**KEY RESULTS** <br>
* Custom curriculum development based on specific client needs across all ages and levels (i.e., preparing staff for international audits, product presentations). <br>
* Designed custom cultural immersion programs for Chinese employees transferring to the UK (covering practical skills and key cultural differences). <br>
* Delivered and refined training content for a large food production company to prepare staff for critical tasks like hosting international audits.<br>
* Translated, proofread, and edited client specific material from between Mandarin Chinese and Dutch & English.<br>
